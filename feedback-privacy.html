Feedback privacy

Anonymous product feedback.

Rookline feedback has no name or email field. The feedback service receives only the ratings you choose, your free-text comment, the app or site version, and a retry-protection identifier.

What we do not collect

Feedback has no structured name or email field. It does not include driving telemetry, session recordings, setup files, vehicle or track identity, wheelbase data, RookForce data, or AI-provider credentials.

Comments

Please do not add passwords, payment details, private files, or other sensitive information. If you type an email address in a comment, the feedback service receives that text before replacing direct email-like text with [redacted email] for publication. This is an automated safeguard, not a guarantee that a comment contains no identifying detail.

Public tracker

Sending feedback creates a public issue in the Rookline Feedback tracker on GitHub. Other identifying detail in a free-text comment can still be published. Public content can be copied, quoted, or indexed before it is removed.

Retention

Published feedback issues are deleted automatically after 30 days. Because entries have no structured identity and there is no individual lookup, there is no manual deletion-request path. Deletion cannot remove copies, quotes, or search indexes created while the issue was public.

This notice applies when feedback is enabled in a published Rookline release or on this site.
