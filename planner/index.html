Sources & forecast notes

Schedule and weather configuration from LMU Portal, a community schedule. This page works with LMU closed and checks the source every 30 seconds while running. Race starts less than 30 minutes away count down in minutes and seconds.

Forecast timing divides the published session length into five equal parts, using temperature targets. These are planning points, not exact rain-onset times or live in-session measurements. Configured wind is displayed in km/h; compass letters describe the published direction. Missing weather stays unavailable.
