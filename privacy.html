ROOKLINE WEBSITE

Privacy &
site information.

This public website introduces the Rookline tools and shows the community-published LMU schedule. Browsing it does not connect to your game, telemetry, Steam account or RaceHub.

What your browser uses

Schedules stores your display theme and timezone in your browser. The public schedule is cached for this browser tab to avoid repeating requests as you change pages. Schedule requests go directly to LMU Portal's public schedule service while Home or Schedules is open. GitHub hosts the website and receives normal web requests. External community, download and source links lead to their respective services.

Feedback and ideas are welcome in the Rookline Discord community. Review the service’s privacy information before sharing personal details.

Weather & artwork

Forecasts describe published event settings, not live measured game weather. Missing information remains unavailable. Product pages retain their asset acknowledgments; Schedules weather artwork attribution is available here.
