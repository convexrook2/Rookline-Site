ROOKLINE WEBSITE

Privacy &
site information.

This public website introduces the Rookline tools and shows the community-published LMU schedule. Browsing it does not connect to your game, telemetry, Steam account or RaceHub.

What your browser uses

The Planner stores your display theme and timezone in your browser. Schedule requests go directly to LMU Portal's public schedule service while the Planner is open. GitHub hosts the website and receives normal web requests. External community, download and source links lead to their respective services.

The Rookline feedback form has its own feedback privacy notice. Submitted feedback can become a public issue; review that notice before sending.

Weather & artwork

Forecasts describe published event settings, not live measured game weather. Missing information remains unavailable. Product pages retain their asset acknowledgments; Planner weather artwork attribution is available here.
