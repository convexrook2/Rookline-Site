Rookline Drive

Set up your cockpit

Install Drive on Windows and open it from Start. With HUD output set to Automatic, Drive follows the mode LMU is running in. Your desktop and VR layouts are saved separately.

Download Drive 0.6.50

In SteamVR

  1. Start SteamVR and open Drive. Select Rookline Drive in the SteamVR dashboard. You can set up before entering the car.
  2. Choose Edit HUD layout. Point at a panel, hold the trigger to move it, then release. Use the thumbstick up or down to resize it and left or right to change its distance. Panels can be flat or curved.
  3. Select Save placement, look for Saved, then choose Done editing. In the car, recenter while parked in your normal driving position.
View the panel placement diagram ↗

On a monitor

  1. Enter LMU’s driving view. In Drive choose Show game HUD.
  2. Right-click the Drive icon in the Windows notification area and choose Edit game HUD. Drag panels to position them, or drag an edge to resize.
  3. Choose Save & drive. Drive hides the desktop overlay in menus and the garage.

From the wheel

  1. Open Settings → Controls. Choose Bind beside Previous or Next HUD view and press a wheel button or key.
  2. Under HUD panels → Views in HUD cycle, choose your views and which panels appear in each. Select Save views.
  3. In the car, cycle through your views. Clear hides all panels. Previous or Next brings them back to their saved positions.

Where to find it

Window or missing panels
Open Rookline Drive from its tray icon. In VR, use Show panels, cycle out of Clear or choose Refresh HUD. Edit mode labels each movable panel.
Reading the map
Cycle All cars, Your class and Follow your car. Choose Full fidelity and enable wetness to read surface differences. Map guide ↗
Contact reports
Open Contacts in the companion or SteamVR dashboard. Choose a session to review vehicle contacts, environment impacts and observed damage.
Colours, backgrounds and updates
Use Settings → Appearance for colour vision and panel opacity. About → Application checks for updates and keeps your saved settings.

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, 64-bit. The installer includes .NET. For VR, start SteamVR, including when using OpenXR through SteamVR. Release details ↗
