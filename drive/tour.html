Six stops. Your own cockpit.

HOW DO YOU DRIVE?

01 / OPEN DRIVE

Start your engines.

Open Rookline Drive from Windows Start, then enter LMU’s driving view.
Start SteamVR, open Rookline Drive, then enter LMU in VR.
Install & compatibility

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, 64-bit. The installer includes .NET; WebView2 is required. The same Start menu shortcut selects desktop or VR for LMU. OpenXR uses SteamVR.

Download Drive 0.6.47 ↗

02 / SHOW THE HUD

Meet your cockpit.

Choose Show game HUD in Drive.
Open Drive in the SteamVR dashboard to manage your HUD.
Panels not showing?

Enter LMU’s driving view. In desktop mode the overlay hides in menus and the garage. If your HUD view is Clear, use Previous or Next HUD view to bring the panels back.

03 / PLACE PANELS

Find your space.

Right-click Drive’s tray icon → Edit game HUD.
SteamVR dashboard → Edit HUD layout.
  1. 1Drag a panel to move it.
  2. 2Drag an edge or corner to resize it.
Choose your panels

Use the editor’s toolbar checkboxes. Leave space for any LMU panels you want to keep.

  1. 1Point at a panel; hold the trigger to move it.
  2. 2Release the trigger to place it.
Size & distance

While editing, thumbstick up/down changes size; right brings the panel closer and left moves it farther away. Keep the SteamVR dashboard open.

04 / SAVE LAYOUT

Save. Go racing.

Choose Save & drive. Your desktop layout is ready.
Choose Save placement, look for Saved, then Done editing.
What gets saved?

Desktop and VR placements are separate. In VR, one save keeps all moved panels. Done editing or closing the dashboard does not automatically save. In the desktop editor, Cancel discards the current edit.

05 / READ THE MAP

Know the road ahead.

Choose All cars, Your class or Follow your car.
What am I looking at?

Numbered markers show traffic. Follow your car keeps your direction pointing up. Surface colours estimate changing conditions; they are not a water-depth measurement at every point.

Watch the recorded weather examples ↗

06 / BIND A BUTTON

One button. A clearer view.

Settings → Controls → HUD navigation. Bind Previous and Next HUD view.
Bind & choose your views

Choose Bind, release any held input, then press your wheel button or key. Choose the views in Settings → HUD panels → Views in HUD cycle, then Save views. Clear hides all panels; Previous or Next brings them back.

You’re ready. Explore Drive ↗

Choose Contacts in Drive, or open Contacts in the VR dashboard.

↓ Updates

Settings → About → Application. Your saved settings and layout are preserved.
